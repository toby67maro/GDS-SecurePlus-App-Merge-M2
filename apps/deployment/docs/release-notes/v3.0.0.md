**Secure Plus – Modern Work 3.0.** Experience for every group of users, a schedule-true roadmap, project plans, SOWs, six heat maps, interactive charts, 34 themes and GDS branding. Published as a pre-release because the build is not code-signed.

### Downloads
- `GDS-SecurePlusAgent-3.0.0-win-x64-Setup.exe` – installer (recommended). Upgrades 2.x; no schema change. The installer, desktop shortcut and taskbar now show the GDS icon.
- `...-portable.zip`, `...-framework-dependent.zip` – engineering builds.
- `...-SHA256SUMS.txt` – SHA-256 checksums.

### Menus
- Order: **Dashboard, Assessment, Deployment, Reporting, Workspace, Settings**. Workspace has Customer Profiles, Specification Profiles, Tenant App Registration and Exceptions; Settings is its own menu.
- **Advanced** (Data Explorer, Diagnostics) appears after **Settings > Application Settings > Enable Advanced Mode** with the passcode entered in the box next to it.
- Menus slide open, pages glide in, tiles and charts lift on hover. Text anywhere can be selected and copied (right-click: Copy / Copy All), including the Activity panel.

### Experience
- **Workspace > Customer Profiles > User Groups**: up to 25 groups per customer (executives, frontline workers, international travellers, administrators, contractors…), each starting from an experience profile. *Add Suggested Groups* adds the common ones.
- **Assessment > Experience Configuration**: plain-language choices a CTO or IT lead makes, by Microsoft solution (sign-in and MFA, locations, personal devices, updates, encryption, admin rights, USB, email, sharing, Teams, labels and DLP, Copilot…), for All Users or one group. Shows security and friction scores live and flags choices that conflict with the compliance targets. **Import EaaS Form Results** turns the discovery and design-workshop forms into groups, targets and answers.
- **Reporting > Experience Map**: each group's experience in detail; switch on NIST, CMMC, HIPAA, PCI, CIS, ISO, Essential Eight, SOX or ITAR to see what must change, the conflicts, the least disruptive fix and how friction changes — so customers see why one group's experience can't meet another need.

### Roadmap, Project Plan and SOW
- **Roadmap**: a tile for every phase; dates come from the effort hours on Monday–Friday 8-hour days from the start date you pick (with the number of engineers); phases collapsed on first open. Work is ordered by dependencies, then by the most risk reduced and compliance gained per hour. A left-to-right **flow timeline** shows work packages with arrows from each dependency; charts show hours by phase, items by severity and value delivered by week. Filter by phase, severity, status, solution area or text; choose what to show; export HTML/CSV/JSON/text or the timeline as an image.
- **Deployment > Project Plan**: build from the GDS work breakdown structure and/or the roadmap, edit tasks, hours, roles and predecessors, see the schedule, save (dlv.ProjectPlan) and export.
- **Assessment > SOW**: pick or create a template (saved in dlv.SowTemplate) and fill it from the customer, user groups and their experience, the latest assessment, the roadmap and a project plan; save as HTML or Word.

### Insights
- **Heat Map**: Compliance, Remediation Priority (severity × effort), Fleet Compliance, Compliance Trend, Threat Coverage (ATT&CK tactic × service tier) and Experience Friction — click any cell for its detail.
- **Dashboard** and **Fleet View**: compliance gauge, status and severity donuts, compliance by solution area, score trend and fleet charts; click to drill down.
- **Catalog > Remediation Options**: Graph REST and PowerShell, JSON, Settings Catalog / OMA-URI, Group Policy, registry (.reg and PowerShell), portal steps and CIS/Microsoft baseline procedures from the knowledge base. Section headers are readable in dark themes.

### Themes and Branding
- 34 themes, including Light, Medium, Dark, System, GDS Light, GDS Dark, Aurora (Copilot-inspired), Clay (Claude-inspired), Blueprint (Visio-inspired), Visio diagram-theme inspired themes and High Contrast. Pick in the header or in **Settings > Appearance > Theme Gallery**.
- GDS logo in the header (navy or white by theme), GDS icon in the title bar, taskbar, shortcuts, Programs and Features and the installer.

### Known Issues
- Experience scores, framework notes, SOW templates and scoring weights are drafts for GDS review (`docs/DECISIONS.md`).
- Roadmap ordering is a deterministic optimiser; AI-assisted ordering would send customer data to an AI service and waits for your approval.
- Rates, customer variables and live deployment are not included (rate card encrypted; live tenant writes not approved). See `docs/DOCUMENTS_AUDIT.md`.
- No writes to customer tenants. Unsigned build: SmartScreen may warn.
