**Secure Plus – Modern Work 2.2.0.** Knowledge base phase 1: the Secure Controls Framework, NIST SP 800-53 / 800-53A and MITRE ATT&CK are loaded into the SecurePlus database and linked to the Secure Plus control catalog. Published as a pre-release because the build is not code-signed.

### Downloads
- `GDS-SecurePlusAgent-2.2.0-win-x64-Setup.exe` – installer (recommended). Upgrades 2.1; no schema change.
- `...-portable.zip`, `...-framework-dependent.zip` – engineering builds.
- `...-SHA256SUMS.txt` – SHA-256 checksums of every download.

### After Upgrading
The knowledge base loads in the background the first time a computer with 2.2 connects (about a minute on SQL Server; watch the activity panel). It is loaded once per database: other computers see it immediately. **Settings > Database Health > Data Coverage** shows the version and has **Reload Knowledge Base**.

### Database
- **Secure Controls Framework 2026.3**: 1,591 SCF controls in 34 domains with weight, PPTDF, CSF function, control question, solutions by business size, SP-CMM maturity criteria (9,546), compensating controls (2,794), change history; 12 SCF CORE sets; 6,446 assessment objectives; 422 evidence request list artifacts linked to their controls.
- **270 focal documents** (NIST, ISO, CIS, PCI DSS, HIPAA, CMMC, SOC 2, GDPR, NIS2, DORA, Essential Eight and more) with 76,023 SCF-to-requirement mappings.
- **NIST SP 800-53 Rev 5** full control text and discussion; **NIST SP 800-53A** assessment objectives with examine, interview and test methods (4,850).
- **Risks and threats**: the SCF risk (65) and threat (45) catalogs and the likelihood of each when a control fails (175,010 ratings on a Unlikely / Possible / Likely scale).
- **MITRE ATT&CK Enterprise**: 15 tactics, 706 techniques and sub-techniques, 44 mitigations, 1,568 technique-mitigation links and 4,877 SCF-control-to-technique links.
- **Catalog links**: all 85 framework references used by the Secure Plus catalog (NIST 800-53, CIS v8.1, ISO 27001, CMMC 2.0, PCI DSS 4.0.1, HIPAA) are linked to their SCF counterparts, so every catalog control reaches SCF controls, risks, threats and ATT&CK techniques.
- Versioned and idempotent: reference rows are updated in place (IDs never change), only the knowledge base's own links are replaced.

### Known Issues
- The SCF workbook does not give the relationship type of each mapping; they are stored as "Intersects With" (see `docs/DECISIONS.md`).
- Later phases (CIS benchmark PDFs, Microsoft baselines, GDS standards, question bank, WBS) and the CTID, licence-capability, service tier, SOW and scoring documents are still to come; `docs/DATA_COVERAGE.md` lists the 132 tables that remain empty.
- No writes to customer tenants. Unsigned build: SmartScreen may warn.
